ABOUT ME

I care about good software, but I’m not only my job.

I’m a software engineer based in Canada. I like building products that make complicated work feel simpler, and I’m happiest when I can follow a feature from the interface through to the systems behind it.

Travel is the other big part of my life. I love travelling solo because it keeps me curious, adaptable and open to new experiences. I have been to 26 countries solo, and 31 in total. I have a travel blog in the works to document my solo trips, and I’m always planning the next one. My favourite place in the world is Philippines, and I am always hoping to return there to explore more.

Scuba diving is where everything slows down. I love the calm of being underwater, the feeling of entering a world most people rarely see, and the mix of preparation and adventure that comes with every dive.

Those parts of my life shape how I work too: I adapt quickly, stay calm when plans change, and genuinely enjoy learning unfamiliar systems.
